DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE FOR TESTING)

This agreement is made between Example Retail GmbH (the "Controller") and CloudHost Services Ltd (the "Processor").

1. Subject matter and duration
1.1 The Processor hosts the Controller's online shop and processes personal data on behalf of the Controller for this purpose.
1.2 The processing lasts for the term of the main services agreement.
1.3 The personal data concerned are customer names, email addresses, delivery addresses and order histories of the Controller's customers.

2. Instructions
2.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country.
2.2 If the Processor is required by law to process data otherwise, it shall inform the Controller before processing, unless that law prohibits this.

3. Confidentiality
3.1 The Processor ensures that all persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by a duty of confidentiality.

4. Security
4.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as described in Annex 1.

5. Sub-processors
5.1 The Processor may engage sub-processors at its own discretion and will publish a list of them on its website.

6. Data subject requests
6.1 The Processor shall assist the Controller, by appropriate technical and organisational measures, in responding to requests from data subjects.

7. Personal data breaches
7.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

8. Term and termination
8.1 After the end of the services the Processor may retain the personal data for as long as it considers necessary for its business purposes.

9. International transfers
9.1 The Processor may transfer personal data to its affiliates outside the European Economic Area.
