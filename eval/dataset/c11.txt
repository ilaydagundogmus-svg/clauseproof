DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT C11)

This agreement is made between Example Retail GmbH (the "Controller") and CloudHost Services Ltd (the "Processor") and supplements the main services agreement between them (the "Main Agreement"). All names are fictitious.

1. Fees
1.1 The Controller pays the fees set out in the Main Agreement.

2. Subject matter
2.1 The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller in connection with the services under the Main Agreement.

3. Instructions
3.1 The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organisation, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which the Processor is subject; in such a case the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

4. Confidentiality
4.1 The Processor's management is bound by confidentiality obligations in respect of the Controller's data.

5. Security of processing
5.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, as required by Article 32 GDPR, and as described in Annex II (including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls and regular testing).

6. Sub-processors
6.1 The Processor shall inform the Controller of any new sub-processor it engages.

7. Liability
7.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is governed by the provisions of the Main Agreement.

8. Data subject requests
8.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling the Controller's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Chapter III GDPR.

9. Breaches
9.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay.

10. Deletion or return
10.1 At the choice of the Controller, the Processor shall delete or return all personal data to the Controller after the end of the provision of the services and delete existing copies within 30 days, unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data.

11. Compliance information
11.1 The Processor shall provide the Controller, on request, with a copy of its current ISO 27001 certificate.

12. Unlawful instructions
12.1 The Processor shall immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection provisions.

13. International transfers
13.1 Transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area are permitted subject to appropriate safeguards.

14. Governing law
14.1 This DPA is governed by German law. The courts of Munich have exclusive jurisdiction.
